# PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

## Vision
Xspeeria is a trusted P2P fiat currency exchange platform.

## MVP
- Authentication
- KYC
- FX Marketplace
- Matching Engine
- Settlement
- Transaction Timeline
- Notifications

## Primary Users
- Diaspora
- Freelancers
- SMEs
- Importers

## First Corridor
NGN ⇄ GBP (Year 1 pilot). NGN ⇄ USD is the Year 2 corridor, per the 5-Year Business Plan's corridor expansion roadmap (Section 8). This corrects the original NGN ⇄ USD-only framing. **Amended 2026-10-19 (DEC-050, ADR-043): NGN ⇄ USD joins NGN ⇄ GBP at the MVP. Amended 2026-11-09 (DEC-112, ADR-100): NGN ⇄ CAD also joins at the MVP.**

## Pricing Model
Flat coordination fee per side of a matched transaction (illustratively 7.5x the local instant-transfer cost), not a percentage-of-transaction fee. See 5-Year Business Plan, Section 5.

## Success Metrics
- Successful matches
- Settlement completion
- User retention
